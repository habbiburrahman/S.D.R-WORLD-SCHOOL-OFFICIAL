ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Learning with purpose.

A welcoming place to learn, discover talents and build a strong foundation for life.

​ Why choose S.D.R. World School?

Student-Centered Approach: We recognize that every child learns differently. Our dedicated educators mentor each student to help them reach their full potential

Innovative Infrastructure: Safe, modern classrooms, well-equipped science and technology labs, and spacious athletic facilities.

​Safe & Supportive Atmosphere: A warm, inclusive community where every child feels valued, safe, and encouraged to excel.

​Strong Home-School Partnership :We actively engage parents in their child’s educational journey, building a collaborative foundation for success.
