ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Learning with purpose.

A welcoming place to learn, discover talents and build a strong foundation for life.

At S.D.R WORLD SCHOOL, our goal is to support students through meaningful learning, positive values and opportunities to grow. We aim to encourage independent thinking, creativity, responsibility and respect for others.

Our school community works together to help each learner build confidence and develop the skills needed for tomorrow.
